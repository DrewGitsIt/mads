#2  “Active” / “Earlier” vs “pause” / “resume” rename, don't restructure the grouping is sound; the words are overloaded

The structure is not the problem. There are two orthogonal facts about a session, and today they share a vocabulary — so a sentence like “this session is active” has two different answers depending on which one you meant.

axis 1  Recency — when did it last reply?

A continuous value, folded once: isEarlier() puts a session in the collapsed tail when its last reply is ≥ 24h old. Nothing else. It never changes because of anything you click.

The proposal — pick one word per axis and use it everywhere

The deeper point about ⏸/▶: pause and resume are not what they do. Pause/resume implies work suspended mid-flight that resuming continues. Nothing is suspended — the conversation is durable on disk either way, and SessionRail.tsx's own comment says a closed session “wakes transparently on the first composer send”. So ⏸ frees a process and ▶ pre-warms one. Neither is a state you must manage, which is why asking “what does pausing mean?” has no satisfying answer: it is an answer to a question about memory, dressed as an answer to a question about work.

The stronger option, if you want it: delete ▶. It is a latency optimisation with no other user-visible effect, and the first message wakes the session anyway. Removing a control costs nothing to explain; keeping it costs a tooltip, a disabled state, a menu item and this conversation. It stays in the workspace kebab for the rare deliberate pre-warm.

And the structural question worth naming: railGroups.ts says the filter chips “replace the old Active/Recent grouping” — but the Earlier fold survived that change, so the rail now has two grouping mechanisms. They do different jobs (chips are triage you choose; the fold is 24h of clutter going away without you choosing anything), so I would keep both. But if #2 still feels muddy after the rename, this is the thing to cut, not the words.

#3  The hover detail panel one root cause, both symptoms it is the native title attribute

SessionRail.tsx:982 is title={tooltip || undefined}, and railTooltip() builds a \n-joined string. So that panel is Chromium's own tooltip — which explains both halves of your complaint at once: it is styled by the browser and cannot be styled by us at all, and it is not a DOM node, so there is nothing in it to select.

Same facts, in the app's own chrome — the Popover primitive the launchpad pills and the link menus already use, so it inherits --bg2, --line, --r-sm, the shadow, and outside-click/Escape dismissal for free. Paths in mono, which the title string could never do.

Trigger — the one real decision here

Selectable text and hover-dismissal are in direct conflict. A surface that vanishes when the pointer leaves the card cannot be one you drag a selection across. So either the panel gets an explicit trigger, or it needs hover-intent logic (open after a delay, stay open while the pointer is inside the panel itself, close on leaving both). I would not build the second to get the first.
