One modal for linking: create, inspect, re-tune, mute

Every card shows the same chain-link button, linked or not. Clicking it blurs the conversation pane and opens one modal over it. The rail stays live while the modal is open: click cards to add or remove them, and the modal updates as you go. If the card is already linked, the modal opens with its whole group loaded. Cadence is Follow, Signals, or Muted (manual_only), set per subordinate.
Try it: hover a card, click 🔗. Start with Fix deploy… (a leader with one muted subordinate) or Profile startup time (not linked).

What each piece is doing

  1. One button, one meaning. The ⇲/⇱ pair and the "Choose mode / Cancel" bar are gone. The chain button sits on every card. When the card is in a link group it turns accent-colored and shows a count.
  2. Rail stays interactive; the conversation pane is blurred. The modal never covers the rail, so you choose sessions the same way as today. Checkboxes and the accent edge on the rail show that clicking adds a session. Sessions from a different workspace are dimmed and shake if clicked.
  3. Role first, then cadence. Picking Leader or Monitor opens the default-cadence choice. Each subordinate can then override it, which is how you mute one without touching the others.
  4. Re-opening loads the group. On a leader, you get its group. On a subordinate, you get its leader's group with that row highlighted, plus a "from …" chip saying where you came from.
  5. Swap direction (⇅ on a row). If you started from the session that should be the subordinate, promote another session to leader without starting over.
  6. Save is a diff. In edit mode the button reads "Save 2 changes" and stays disabled until something changes. Removing a row unlinks it. "Unlink all" removes the whole group.

Open questions to align on

  1. A subordinate with two holders (watched by two monitors, say). The mock opens the first holder's group. Options: holder tabs at the top of the modal, or a one-step "which group?" picker.
  2. A session that is both a leader and a subordinate. The mock shows its own group (it as leader). Should the "led by …" relationship also appear as a read-only line?
  3. One role per group. The modal forces a single role per leader: all Leader or all Monitor. The data model allows a mix. Fine to drop that in the UI?
  4. Mute mark on the rail card (toggle above). Is muted state worth showing in the rail, or only in the modal?
  5. Esc / click on the blurred pane: discard edits silently, or confirm when there are unsaved changes?
  6. Vocabulary: "Follow / Signals / Muted" replaces "Follow intently / Wake on signals / Catch up on request". The longer descriptions stay in the drawer.
