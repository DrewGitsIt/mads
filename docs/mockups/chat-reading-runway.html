Reading runway: three ways the history can behave while a reply streams

Same reply, same pace, three scroll policies. Press Send and read along in each pane. “Moves” counts separate scroll motions. Mock column is narrower than the real one, so lines wrap more often than in the app. At 250 wpm, today’s pane jumps about once every 3–4 s.

Pinned to the bottom. Each wrapped line moves the whole history up by one line height, right away.

2 · Glide

Still follows the bottom, but eases there over about half a second. No jumps, a slow steady drift instead. The smallest change to the code.

moves continuous

3 · Runway (your idea)

On send, your message slides to the top and the turn reserves a screen of space under it. The reply fills downward and the history stays still until the text reaches the bottom.

moves 0

What to look for

  • Runway, page turn: when the text reaches the bottom edge, one smooth scroll brings the last three lines up near the top, giving a new runway. That's about one move per screen of text.
  • Runway, stop + pill: the history never moves by itself after the first slide. The reply keeps revealing below the edge, and the pill (or scrolling down) brings it into view. Calmest option, but you have to act to keep reading.
  • Tool cards take up runway like text does. In a long agentic turn the runway fills with tool rows, then pages.
  • After the turn, Keep: the space stays until your next send. Collapsing it the moment the turn ends would pull the view down, the same jump we're removing.
  • After the turn, Trim off-screen: only space below the bottom edge is removed, so nothing you can see moves. Scroll up a little after the turn and scroll back down: the blank is gone.
  • After the turn, Settle on type: click into the composer under the runway pane and the reply slides down to sit just above it.
  • Reduce motion (mads follows the system setting or its own Appearance toggle): Snap makes every move instant, settle included. Fade dips the pane out for about 120ms, moves it, and dips it back in, so nothing visibly travels. Snap, no settle keeps the blank (trim still removes the off-screen part). Try Short + Trim + settle, then click the composer.
  • Unchanged in all three: scrolling up still lets go, and scrolling back to the bottom picks it up again. Reduce motion swaps the smooth scrolls for instant ones.
