Turn strip: a capped, scrolling window

The turn columns get at most half the strip; the turn's prompt always keeps the other half. Turns out of view fade at that edge, and a count sits under the arrow on that side. Click a turn, shift-click for a range, and use ‹ › to step. At an edge the window moves one column. Drag the Tab width slider to see a narrow window.

Long session 140 turns: opens at turn 1, nothing selected

  • Counts: a turn counts as out of view once more than half of its column is past the edge.
  • Arrows: with nothing selected, › picks the leftmost visible turn and ‹ the rightmost, so the first press never jumps out of view.
  • Wheel: a vertical mouse wheel over the strip scrolls it sideways; trackpads scroll it natively.

Short session 9 turns: no window, no counts, no fade
